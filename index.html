How to Generate 3D Cartoon Videos

Follow three simple steps to produce watermark-free vertical or horizontal videos.

1. Prompt or Reference Image

Describe your 3D character and scene in English for pure Text-to-Video, or upload a character reference image to animate it directly.

2. Choose Voice & Dialogue

Enter your spoken dialogue in Hindi, Bhojpuri, English, or regional Indian languages and pick a custom multi-age character voice.

3. Select Ratio & Download

Pick 9:16 for Reels/Shorts or 16:9 for YouTube, render talking motion with camera zoom, and download your watermark-free video.
